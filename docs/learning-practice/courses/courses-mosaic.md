# Courses and learning tracks

## Track ownership

Zipeek exposes separate learning tracks for Vocabulary, Grammar and Pinyin. Each track owns its curriculum content, learning activities, route-specific interaction state and progress semantics. Shared layout and mosaic utilities do not make the tracks share a single scoring engine.

## Mosaic presentation

Course mosaics use the application's Chinese-inspired visual style with readable regions and a clear selected/locked state. The selected region must have a visible label and accessible control. The previously retired static course mosaic reference is **not** an approved fidelity target; current working UI and the latest approved screenshot-driven requirements control new visual work.

Regions may remain visible but locked until the learner meets the qualifying evidence rules. A locked region is not an invitation to generate or record unearned proficiency. Locked state must explain how progress is earned and remain accessible to keyboard and screen-reader users.

## Learning evidence

Grammar proficiency requires ten qualifying successes per pattern, distributed over at least seven distinct days within a rolling fourteen-day window, drawn from supported Translate Sentence, Sentence Builder and Sentence Transformation submissions. Mere lesson visits, draft examples or unscored interactions do not qualify. Vocabulary exposure and visitation must be distinguished from acquisition, and progress is derived from persisted evidence. See [learning evidence](../practice/learning-evidence.md).

## Curriculum and content boundaries

Teaching drafts, published course routes and assessed completion are different states. The presence of a curriculum record, route, generated lesson or practice sample is not sufficient evidence of an authored, validated, published assessment. Grammar examples generated by an optional AI service must be labelled as examples rather than scoring evidence. The application must show capability-aware service failures and keep personal credentials in their approved service boundary.

Course content design, registry updates and validation must follow [authoring](../../engineering/courses-authoring.md), [curriculum registry](courses-curriculum-registry.md), [HSK alignment](hsk-grammar-alignment.md), and [acceptance](../../testing-quality/acceptance/approved-behavior-acceptance.md).

## Source and verification

Inspect the source's course components and pedagogical crates for actually published slugs, lesson steps, region layouts and persistence before claiming implementation. Earlier route snapshots, historical asset directions and content inventory observations are preserved in [archived course source](../../archive-history/audits/superseded-maintained/learning-practice--courses--courses-mosaic-source.md).
